Predicts expected return on ad spend and revenue contribution for every marketing channel — giving teams the performance benchmarks they need to prioritise investment decisions before campaigns launch, not after they miss targets.

Generates a detailed month-by-month allocation across every spend category: ads, retention, SEO, social, tools, content production, and testing reserves. Full 12-month visibility means no budgeting surprises mid-year and simplified financial reporting for every stakeholder.

Enterprise-Grade Financial Governance

Enforces strict validation rules across percentage totals, budget reconciliation, allocation consistency, and numerical accuracy. Built for agencies, enterprise marketing teams, and growth platforms that can't afford planning inconsistencies.

Content production spend isn't allocated as a flat percentage — it's dynamically adjusted based on your publishing cadence, format mix, and content strategy inputs. A business publishing daily video content gets a materially different production budget than one running a monthly long-form blog. The system understands the difference.
